Ask HR: FAQ

Nine to Fur (@nine2fur) is a funny animated office comedy for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Short clips about the commute, meetings, rent day and salary day, starring felt and knit designer-toy characters. Made by Syed in Bengaluru, India.

What is Nine to Fur?

Nine to Fur is a funny animated office comedy: short clips about office life and adulting, made for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. The commute, meetings, rent day and salary day, played by tactile felt and knit characters.

Who is in Nine to Fur?

Nine to Fur stars Syed, a new hire chasing The Window Desk, plus three felt coworkers: Mo, a tired, deadpan capybara; Pip, a chaotic, overconfident pigeon; and Ms. Hoot, the owl boss who is always on a call. Syed is a real person. The others are mostly felt.

Where can I watch Nine to Fur?

Nine to Fur clips are on YouTube Shorts at youtube.com/@nine2fur and on Instagram Reels at instagram.com/nine2fur. The handle is @nine2fur everywhere, so there is only one thing to remember.

How often are new Nine to Fur clips posted?

Nine to Fur is new: the first clips are coming soon, and new clips drop weekly after that. Follow @nine2fur on YouTube or Instagram to catch the first one. Ms. Hoot has scheduled a call about the schedule.

Is Nine to Fur made with AI?

Yes, Nine to Fur is made with AI tools and human direction from its creator, Syed. The felt characters are digital, so nobody had to knit Ms. Hoot a cardigan.

Who makes Nine to Fur?

Nine to Fur is made by Syed, a creator based in Bengaluru, India, who also plays the lead. On screen he is a new hire who believes one perfect week earns the desk by the window. HR says they will talk about it.
