Ask HR: FAQ

Nine to Fur (@nine2fur) is a funny office comedy with no dialogue, for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Real Indian IT office moments, played by felt and knit designer-toy coworkers and one real new hire. Made by Syed in Bengaluru, India.

What is Nine to Fur?

Nine to Fur is a funny office comedy with no dialogue. Each video takes one real Indian IT office moment, like the meeting, the chai break or “one by two”, and plays it like old-school slapstick in colour: big expressions, music and exaggerated sound effects. Every video runs about two minutes and is cut into Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

Who is in Nine to Fur?

Nine to Fur stars Syed, the lead: an earnest, polite new hire who tries a little too hard. His three felt coworkers are Pip, an eager pigeon who makes everything escalate; Mo, a deadpan capybara who is somehow always already there; and Ms. Hoot, a serene owl boss whose slow head turn freezes the room. Syed is a real person. The others are mostly felt.

Where can I watch Nine to Fur?

Nine to Fur clips are on YouTube Shorts at youtube.com/@nine2fur and on Instagram Reels at instagram.com/nine2fur. The handle is @nine2fur everywhere, so there is only one thing to remember.

How often are new Nine to Fur clips posted?

Nine to Fur is new: the first video is coming soon, and new clips drop weekly after that. Follow @nine2fur on YouTube or Instagram to catch it. Ms. Hoot has scheduled a call about the schedule.

What is EP01 about?

EP01 is “The Meeting & The Chai Meeting”, coming soon. The same meeting happens twice: polite in the conference room, honest at the chai stall. The guest stars are Anna the chai-wala, a felt elephant, and a very tall, very polite team of giraffe clients.

Does Nine to Fur have dialogue?

No. Nine to Fur has no spoken dialogue, only big expressions, music and exaggerated sound effects, so it works in any language and with the sound off. The sound is half the fun, though.

Is Nine to Fur made with AI?

Yes, Nine to Fur is made with AI tools and human direction from its creator, Syed. The felt characters are digital, so nobody had to knit Ms. Hoot a cardigan.

Who makes Nine to Fur?

Nine to Fur is made by Syed, a creator based in Bengaluru, India, who also plays the lead: the polite new hire who is perfect in meetings and dramatic at the chai stall. Ms. Hoot says it all sounds good.
